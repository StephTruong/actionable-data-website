Who we are

A hands-on data & AI partner

Actionable Data is a data and AI consultancy founded in Shanghai in 2016. We work with multinational and Chinese companies that have valuable data but no easy way to use it.

We stay from strategy to production: we design the roadmap, build the platform, ship the models and stay accountable for the business result. For companies that need a data leader without a full-time hire, we provide a fractional Head of Data & AI.
